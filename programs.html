What we treat, in detail

Four areas, all worked within what the medical referral allows.

Program 01 · Load, not just pain

Back, neck and joint complaints

Pain tells you something hurts; it does not tell you what the tissue can tolerate. We assess the movement, the range and the load at which the complaint appears, because that is what a treatment plan can actually be built on. Where the pattern does not fit a musculoskeletal complaint, we say so and send you back to the physician.

Program 02 · To the surgeon's protocol

Rehabilitation after surgery or injury

After an operation the limits are not ours to invent: the surgeon sets what may be loaded and when, and we work inside that protocol even when a patient feels ready sooner. Progression is staged against those limits rather than against impatience, and we report back to the referring physician on how the recovery is going.

Program 03 · Hands, then exercise

Manual therapy and mobilisation

Manual therapy can reduce stiffness and give a joint more room, but what holds that gain is the exercise done afterwards. We therefore use it to create a window rather than as a treatment on its own, and we are honest that a patient who only comes for hands-on work tends to return with the same complaint.

Program 04 · Few exercises, done

Exercise therapy and prevention

A programme of twelve exercises gets abandoned in a fortnight; three that fit into an actual day get done. We would rather prescribe less and have it carried out, because between sessions is where most of the recovery happens. We write the programme down and we adjust it when it turns out not to fit your week.
