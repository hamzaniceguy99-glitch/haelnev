How we work

A physiotherapy practice, working inside a medical framework

HA ELNEV is a company incorporated under Belgian law, registered with the Crossroads Bank for Enterprises under enterprise number 0790.464.975, based at 7 Vlierstraat in 3300 Tienen and practising since September 2022. Physiotherapy in Belgium may be carried out only by a practitioner holding a visa from the Federal Public Service Health and an identification number with the national health insurance institute, and the practice holds the professional liability insurance that the law of 22 April 2019 requires.

The limit matters more than the list of treatments. A physiotherapist does not establish a medical diagnosis, does not prescribe medicines, does not request imaging and does not issue sick notes — all of that is reserved to physicians. What we do is assess how a body moves and how much load it tolerates, treat within the referral, and send a patient back to the doctor when the pattern does not fit a musculoskeletal complaint or when something suggests a condition that needs medical examination. Patient rights under the law of 22 August 2002 apply in full, and health data are handled as the special category the GDPR makes them.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Do I need a prescription from my doctor?

Not to be treated, but yes to be reimbursed. You may come without one and pay privately. For a health insurance fund to reimburse part of the cost, Belgian rules require a medical prescription, and we tell you the rate before the first session rather than at the counter afterwards.

Can you tell me what is wrong with my back?

Not as a diagnosis. A physiotherapist does not establish a medical diagnosis in Belgium; that is the physician's role. We can tell you what moves poorly, where the complaint appears under load, and whether it behaves like something physiotherapy helps. If it does not, we send you back to your doctor.

Can you prescribe something for the pain, or order a scan?

No. Prescribing medicines and requesting imaging are reserved to physicians. If we think either is needed we say so and refer you, with a note on what we found, so the consultation starts from something more than a description of the pain.

How many sessions will I need?

We give an honest estimate after the assessment and we reassess as we go, but anyone who promises a fixed number before examining you is guessing. Recovery depends on the condition, on how your body responds and, quite a lot, on the exercises done between sessions.

Will the treatment cure it?

No treatment can be promised an outcome, and we will not do so. We explain what we expect, what would make us change course, and at what point we would conclude that physiotherapy is not the answer for this complaint and refer you on.

Why so few home exercises?

Because a long programme gets abandoned. Three exercises that fit into your actual week get done, and between sessions is where most of the progress happens. If the programme does not fit your week, tell us and we will change it rather than repeat it.
