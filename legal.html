IMPORTANT: a physiotherapist does not establish a medical diagnosis. The diagnosis is made by a physician, and this practice assesses function, movement and load tolerance within that medical framework; where a complaint falls outside the scope of physiotherapy, or where signs suggest a condition requiring medical examination, the patient is referred back to the referring physician or to a doctor without delay. Reimbursement by a health insurance fund requires a medical prescription; treatment given without one is lawful but is charged privately.
